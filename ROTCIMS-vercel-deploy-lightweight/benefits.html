Military & Academic Advantages

Why Complete San Enrique ROTC?

Earn academic credentials, reserve commissions, career advantages, and civic leadership recognition through our comprehensive training program.

Home • Benefits

Graduating from the basic and advance ROTC course awards students tangible academic, civic, and professional advantages recognized by educational and military authorities nationwide.

🎖️

Commissioning & Reserve Rank

Graduates of Basic ROTC enter the Standby/Ready Reserve force as Enlisted Reservists, while Advance ROTC graduates qualify for commissioning as Second Lieutenants in the AFP Reserve.

📜

Civil Service & Merit Advantage

ROTC training certificates enhance credentials when applying for uniformed services (PNP, BFP, BJMP, PCG, AFP) and civil service career opportunities.

🧠

Executive Leadership & Command

Practical leadership development leading platoons and companies, tactical problem solving, high-stress decision making, and team cohesion.

🚑

Disaster Response Certification

First aid, basic life support, search and rescue protocols, and disaster risk reduction management training.
