Admission & Training Requirements

Documentary credentials and medical clearances required before reporting to formation drill assemblies.

Home • Requirements

Before reporting for the first formation drill assembly, all newly enrolled students must submit the required documentary credentials to the Command Staff.

📋

1. Certificate of Registration (COR)

Official copy proving bona fide enrollment in a baccalaureate degree program for the current academic semester.

🩺

2. Medical Clearance & Physical Fitness Certificate

Issued by the University Health Services or a licensed government physician certifying fitness for moderate to rigorous physical training.

✍️

3. Parent / Guardian Consent Waiver

Signed consent form granting permission for tactical formation drills, obstacle courses, and parade rehearsals.

📸

4. 2x2 ID Pictures in Proper Grooming

Two (2) recent colored photographs with white background, military haircut (for males), and neat bun (for females).
