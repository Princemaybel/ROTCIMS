About the San Enrique ROTC Unit

Developing disciplined, patriotic, and competent reserve leaders dedicated to civic responsibility and national defense readiness.

Home • About ROTC

The Reserve Officers' Training Corps (ROTC) program of the San Enrique Campus trains and motivates tertiary students in the art of military science, discipline, civic responsibility, and national defense preparedness pursuant to Republic Act No. 7077 and Republic Act No. 9163.

Mission

To train, equip, and administer enrolled students to produce capable enlisted reservists and commissioned officers for the Armed Forces of the Philippines reserve force.

Vision

To be an exemplary ROTC unit distinguished for tactical discipline, disaster response readiness, civic excellence, and patriotic leadership.

Core Military Values

  • Honor: Adherence to truth, integrity, and ethical conduct in all military actions and academic responsibilities.
  • Duty: Diligence in completing formations, drill instructions, and unit assignments without compromise.
  • Loyalty: Fidelity to the Constitution of the Philippines, the institution, and the chain of command.
  • Courage: Moral fortitude and resilience in overcoming tactical challenges and serving the community.
